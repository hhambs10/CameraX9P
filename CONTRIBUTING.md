# Contributing

CameraX9P is developed by **hambs**. For a bug, include the app version, device model, Android and ColorOS version, the lens and capture profile, and the steps that reproduce it. Use synthetic or otherwise shareable examples where images are needed.

Before submitting changes, run the core checks and Android lint described in the [README](README.md). Identify any third-party material and its license. By submitting original contributions for inclusion, you agree to make them available under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE).
